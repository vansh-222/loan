FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to apply?
Generally, you will need a valid government-issued ID, proof of income (such as recent pay stubs or tax returns), proof of residence, and recent bank statements. Additional documents may be required based on the loan type.
How long does the approval process take?
With our fast online application, you can receive conditional approval within minutes. Full funding typically takes between 24 to 48 hours depending on your bank and the loan type.
Will checking my rates affect my credit score?
No! Using our calculator or getting a pre-qualification quote only requires a soft credit inquiry, which has no impact on your credit score. A hard inquiry is only performed when you officially submit your loan application.
